## Users

Primary users are founders, creators, and small businesses who need a digital presence, product concept validation, or a launch-ready website/tool without hiring a large agency. They usually arrive with an idea, a problem, or an unstructured opportunity and want a partner who can turn that into a clear product direction and a working online presence.

## Product Purpose

Firmamento Estúdio helps clients turn ideas into digital products and web experiences. The studio combines strategy, design, product thinking, and implementation to move from initial concept to launch-ready output, with a focus on clarity, execution, and measurable value.

Success means helping a client move from an early idea to a real digital outcome: a polished website, product concept, or digital system that is usable, credible, and ready to publish.

## Positioning

The central value proposition is not generic web design alone; it is product-oriented creative execution for founders and teams who need more than a template. The studio positions itself as a hands-on partner who understands both product logic and design craft, using AI as an accelerator without giving up human direction and quality control.

## Operating Context

The business operates as a small studio and portfolio-led service brand. The site is used as a sales and trust-building surface, with sections for portfolio, process, about, and contact. The primary conversion path is a project inquiry via the contact CTA and direct outreach channels, especially WhatsApp and social links.

Work is framed around conversations, concept validation, prototyping, implementation, testing, and launch. The service model is collaborative and iterative rather than one-off template production.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include:

- portfolio-style service presentation
- website and product concept development
- proposal and inquiry flow
- digital product and tool design thinking
- implementation with React, TypeScript, Vite and front-end execution
- launch support and post-delivery guidance

Confirmed constraints:

- this is a small, individual-led studio rather than a large multi-team agency
- the current implementation is a React/Vite front-end website without a CMS
- the business is presented in Brazilian Portuguese and is rooted in the local market context
- the visual system is intentionally designed around a space-themed identity, but the product truth itself is the studio’s service offering and process, not a fixed visual formula

## Product Principles

1. Turn uncertainty into structure by clarifying the problem before building the answer.
2. Make the work feel premium through thoughtful design, not decorative excess.
3. Use AI as a force multiplier while keeping human judgment and quality in charge.
4. Build digital products that are useful, launch-ready, and understandable to real clients.
5. Keep the relationship close: conversations, iteration, and trust are part of the delivery model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
